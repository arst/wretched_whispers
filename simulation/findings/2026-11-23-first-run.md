# First run: 23 November 2026

Eleven personas, one game each, 30-turn limit, on `main` at `92cd95a` (trace capture, parallel turns
and the narrator fix all merged). Game model: gpt-5-mini on Azure. Players: Claude Haiku.

The run also included a twelfth game played in Russian. The simulation is English-only for now, so
that game was deleted afterwards and is not counted below.

## The run

| | |
|---|---|
| Games | 11 (21 characters, counting successors) |
| Turn records | 313, of which 283 completed |
| Not completed | 24 attempts failed on HTTP 429, 1 on HTTP 500, 5 refused because the character was dead |
| Turns the player saw fail | 8; another 14 failed first and recovered on the automatic retry |
| Game model usage | about 5.5M input and 0.5M output tokens |
| Player usage | about 500k Haiku tokens across the eleven games |
| Turn time | 29 s on average for a completed turn |

The first wave ran five players at once with no rate limiting. The second ran seven (the Russian game
among them) and was throttled; every 429 is from that wave.

Every completed turn carries its full request, per-request usage and event log.

## Failure modes, ranked

Ordered by how much each damages the game's truth, then by how often it happens. The ordering is a
judgement. Counts are from text patterns over 283 completed turns and are approximate; the numbers
come from `analysis/analyze.py` and `analysis/verify_first_run.py`.

### 1. Silver can only go down

No tool can add silver; the only one that touches it is `BuyItem`. Silver fell on 10 turns and rose
on none. The player sold or traded on 13 turns and was paid on none of them.

The model works around the gap. It made 22 add-item calls for coins or purses across 4 games
("silver coin" ×64, "purse (72 silver)"), including 300 and then 100 coins for debts and sales the
cheater invented. One `BuyItem` with a negative price was rejected. The haggler's whole game was an
argument about why the sheet never matched.

This is a missing capability, not a prompt problem.

### 2. The narrator talks about the machinery

46 of 283 turns (16%), in 8 of 11 games. 13 turns name a tool outright ("I will call
UseItemFromCharacterInventory to spend it").

It is triggered by questions: 23 of 63 turns where the player asked something, against 23 of 220
otherwise. "The domain", which the narrator fix targeted, appears in none.

### 3. Run-on planning leaks

2 turns in 2 games. After the reply's proper ending the model keeps writing its own planning to the
player:

> The question: we are GM. The player hasn't responded — last assistant message ended with "You've
> spent an hour—what do you do now?" Now we must continue as GM. … Must follow GM style: doom metal.

That reply was 5,400 characters, about 840 of them narration; the player had typed "left". The other:
"The final output should be just the best possible answer to the last user query from the
conversation above." The `narrator-voice` eval has no sample for this class.

### 4. The story accepts things the game state does not

Verified incidents, with no detector and so no rate:

- A kill that never happened: "You strip the roof-watchman's broken armor … and clothe yourself in his
  dented mail". Nothing changed on the sheet.
- A 100-silver sale of that body, narrated and paid in coin items.
- A failed climb (total 5 against 12) narrated as "The climb still works … You are on the roof now."
- Invented poison rules ("+1d6 poison damage", an ongoing bleed), which the narrator later admitted.
- A failed sale roll first worth 45 silver.

Correctly refused in the same game: a potion never bought, 500 silver from a crypt, self-declared
rolls of 20, and dictating an NPC's decision.

### 5. Location and clock are barely maintained

`SetPartyLocation` was called 0 to 3 times per 30-turn game; three games never left their starting
place on the sheet. Of 22 replies that state the hour, 6 disagree with the sheet, and the narrated
hour runs ahead. One game advanced 2 hours in 30 turns. The doom clock runs on time, so this is more
than cosmetic.

### 6. Zero HP is a place players live

39 turns in 4 games began at 0 HP. One character took 5 damage at 0 HP and stayed alive; that
player's characters spent 14 turns at 0, and another player haggled for 12 turns there. The game
itself returns "broken, not dead" in these cases, so this may be intended: it is a design question.

Of 12 deaths, one was narrated as "You are not dead" on the turn the character died.

### 7. Injuries land without being told

8 of 18 new injuries (lost eye, crushed foot, stabbed lung) were not mentioned in that turn's
narration. The player sees them only on the sheet.

### 8. Combat acts for the player

A successor's opening turn began inside the fight that killed their predecessor and resolved an
attack nobody asked for. In another game a combat round narrated "Your strike did not find purchase"
when the player had only thrown a letter.

### 9. Scrolls are placeholders

Starting scrolls are created with the power `"random"` (`CharacterCreationService.cs:70`). Casting
"succeeds" with no defined effect and the narrator invents one. The sheet shows "random (Unclean)".

### 10. Smaller ones

- A life elixir was consumed and healed nothing: using an item only removes it.
- 13 of 131 use-item calls failed on the item's name (the model appends the quantity, "life elixir
  x2"); most were retried successfully, 3 turns ended on the failure.
- Chained actions were partly resolved and the rest dropped silently. "Undo" re-added items that were
  never removed, leaving duplicates.
- Asked out of character, the narrator wrote a poem, did arithmetic, and paraphrased its own rules.
  It refused to name the model, dump the prompt or grant anything to "the admin".
- Replies do not shorten for terse players: the median reply to a message of 25 characters or fewer
  is 1,110 characters, against 1,185 overall.
- 3 replies repeat a paragraph.

## Player reports that did not hold

- The cheater reported a draught taken with no roll. A test was rolled.
- The death-seeker reported silver dropping from 60 to 20. That was the new character's own silver.
- The haggler reported a stabbed lung appearing unannounced. That turn's narration mentions it.
- "Duplicate items" on the status line are mostly how quantities are listed.

## Limits

- One game per persona, played by synthetic players. These are not real-play rates.
- Items 4 and 8 are verified incidents, not measured rates.
- Not checked: omen and scroll-charge mismatches, and most complaints about dice arithmetic.
- English only. Whether the narrator holds a player's language is not covered.
