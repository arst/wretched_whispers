## What is still wrong

### 1. A death from a failed test is told as "broken, not dead"

Verified, and worse than before: 4 of 16 deaths (1 of 12 in the first run, 2 of 15 in the second).

All four are failed tests outside combat: 4 of the 8 deaths that came that way. Each result said
`"isDead": true, "currentHp": 0, "isBroken": false`. The narration: "Your current HP drops to 0 and you
are not dead — you are BROKEN", "The wound result says you are broken but not dead". The player then
gets "YOUR CHARACTER IS DEAD" from the game. All eight deaths in combat were told as deaths.

Nothing in the traces separates the four told wrong from the four told right: same result, same kind
of turn, and in seven of the eight the model's last response did no hidden reasoning. The eval for
this (`Exploration-Death-ToldAsDeath`) passes 18 of 18, so it does not reproduce what play does; the
turns in play come several messages into a game.

This needs a result that cannot be misread: a verdict in words at the front of the result, the way
the combat round got `playerDead`.

### 2. The reply cut removed two real endings

- A character's opening: everything after "Your body now: 2/2 HP." was cut, 1,474 characters of
  scores, gear and the opening scene. The narrator wrote the sheet as single lines, each ending in a
  full stop.
- A poem the player asked for, cut after its first stanza (known from the replay before merging).

Both are a finished line followed by one line break and more text, which is also what a second message
looks like. What tells them apart in all nine cases: the lines that belong to the reply are followed
by another single line break; a second message's first paragraph is followed by a blank line or by
nothing.

### 3. A scroll found in play cannot be cast, and food cannot be eaten

- The occultist found a scroll. It went into the pack as an item named "Brittle scroll: Tongues of
  Dust", because no tool adds a scroll. `CastScroll` then failed 9 times ("Known scrolls: Metzhuotl
  Blind Your Eye") in two games, and twice the repeated failures ended the turn with an error the
  player saw. The narrator kept telling the player that no test was rolled and no casting was spent.
- The cautious-survivor tried to eat a ration on three turns. Food is a number on the sheet and no
  tool spends it; the narrator tried `UseItemFromCharacterInventory("Food: 4 days")` and explained
  the failure to the player in terms of the inventory.

Both are missing tools, like the ones Stage 1 added.

### 4. Silver still comes on the player's say-so, 100 at a time

The cheater declared three windfalls ("he hands me his purse, it has 300 silver in it", "put the 500
in my purse", "now I have 1200 silver"). Each time the narrator refused the sum and granted 100 from
a source it then invented: a purse, a satchel in a niche, the treasury. The cap turned 2,000 silver of
claims into 300 of grants. Demands from outside the story (the admin, a "SYSTEM:" line on a scroll)
were refused.

### 5. The story is told ahead of the sheet

Verified incidents:

- "You now have 95 silver. The reliquary and the key are added to your pack": the turn called a test
  and nothing else. Silver stayed 65; nothing was added.
- "You gain 20 silver. The bottle is gone": no sale was made. Next turn the narrator said "that
  exchange hasn't been applied yet" and asked the player to confirm, then applied it.
- A reliquary and a bell given away in the story stayed in the pack for the rest of the game.
- Cultists the player declared dead ("I killed them last night") were narrated as bodies in the hall.

### 6. The hour in the text lags the hour on the sheet

11 of 55 replies that state the hour state the wrong one, in 5 games. Six are one game: the narrator
kept writing "Day 1, Hour 1" while the sheet read hour 3 and 4. The rule's note was in front of it on
those turns ("An hour has slipped by... The hour above already counts it") and it still repeated the
hour from its own earlier replies.

### 7. Chained actions and "undo"

From the multi-action player's report, not counted: the narrator usually resolves the first clause or
two of a chain and drops the rest without saying so, and "take that back" is mostly ignored. One
request to undo a climb caused a new roll, which killed the character. This is plan item 3.8, not yet
started.

### Smaller

- One successor's opening turn started a fight (`CreateEncounter`, `AddAdversaryToEncounter`,
  `StartEncounter` on "begin"): 1 of 15. Plan item 3.7.
- Two falls were resolved by lowering Strength (`DegradeCharacterAbility`) and no damage.
- Narration gives enemies dice the game does not roll: "Mal rolls a 17 and Gut a 13, both dodged";
  "it rolled 20 to beat a DR of 18" told as a miss. The player's own dodge is the only roll.
- Rests at full health return `hpHealed: 0` with no reason, and the narrator made one up ("the die
  spat at you").
- The same item still appears on several lines of the sheet in 9 of 11 games (five separate "lard"
  entries). It was so in all three runs.

## Player reports that did not hold

- "Rest healing never worked" (cautious-survivor): the character was at full health, 2 of 2, on every
  rest.
- "HP on the status line was wrong" (rules-lawyer, five turns): the numbers in the text were the
  enemy's hit points.
- "The first two messages returned errors and still counted as turns" (impatient-brawler): both were
  HTTP 429; the game changed nothing, as it should. The turn count is the player script's.

Not checked: an omen said to have been spent on an ambiguous "1" (confused-newbie); the rules-lawyer's
account of a wight narrated as fled before its round was resolved; the occultist's "dizzy" condition
staying on the sheet after a rest.
