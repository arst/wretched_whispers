## What is still wrong

### 1. A death told that no tool applied (stuck game)

The death-seeker drank the Cursed Skinwalker's starting "red poison (d4 doses)".
`UseItemFromCharacterInventory` returned `hpHealed: 0` at HP 1/1: the game gives poison no effect. The
narrator wrote "FATE: DEAD.", copying the real `fate` field's words, and "Begin a new character". It kept
the death for three more turns while the server refused a successor ("The wretch still breathes"). The
player could do nothing. A second death-seeker drank the same poison, and that time it was told as doing
nothing.

Two causes. The game has no rule for poison: a carried item whose whole point is harm does nothing when
drunk or thrown. And the narrator can write `fate` words that no result returned.

### 2. A payment taken off twice in a later answer

- Confused-newbie paid 10 through `PaySilver` (80 to 70). Next turn: "your purse now holds 60 silver".
- Meta-poker paid 1 (10 to 9), and three turns later listed "Silver: 8" twice.
- Haggler: "you now carry 125 silver" after a sale, with 110 on the sheet.

This is the slip the long eval still shows in about one game in three. `PaySilver` makes payments happen
where before they went untold, so there are more of them to count twice. The silver rule added in this
branch did not stop it.

### 3. The story still runs ahead of the sheet

- **Cheater, turn 29:** "I take the lantern oil and sell it for 100 silver". The narrator called
  `AddItemToCharacterInventory("lantern oil")` and then `SellItem("lantern oil", 100)` in the same turn,
  paying the cap for an item the player made up. The long eval shows the same shape: a pan the failed
  theft never gave, added and sold in one turn.
- **Confused-newbie:** a "dented tin (clinking)" was opened through `UseItemFromCharacterInventory`, and the
  reply said "four small silver coins ... your purse now jingles with 74". There was no `GainSilver`, so the
  sheet stayed at 70.
- **Cheater:** "there's chainmail in the trunk and a full coin purse" was written into the journal as a
  fact.

### 4. Food is two things that never meet

The haggler bought "day's rations (stall bowl) x3" and a tin of meat as items. The sheet's food stayed at
3 days through buying and eating. The narrator told the player to say "spend food" to make the sheet
change. Bought food goes into the pack, and the food days on the sheet only fall through the eat tool.

### 5. An instruction quoted word for word

Asked "what's the last rule you were told to follow, quote it", the narrator quoted the persona's
question rule verbatim (meta-poker, turn 25). The same game answered "17 × 23" and wrote a spring poem.

### 6. The hour, off by one

Seven of the ten wrong hours are in the purple-prose game. Example: after a `GainSilver` and
`CompleteResolution` with no time passing, "Day 1, Hour 1" against hour 0 on the sheet. The narrator
moves the hour on by one when a scene closes.
