## Decisions Made
- **CharacterDto injury fields:** Kept individual boolean fields (HasLostEye, HasBrokenHand, etc.) on CharacterDto for LLM readability. The DTO is what Semantic Kernel's AI sees, and named boolean fields are clearer than a flags integer for an LLM to reason about.
- **Backward-compatible computed properties:** Retained the [JsonIgnore] HasLostEye/HasBrokenHand/etc. properties on Character (added in Plan 02) since CharacterPlugin.CreateCharacterDto uses them for DTO mapping.
- **No custom InjurySet converter needed:** AggregateJsonOptions with JsonStringEnumConverter handles the Flags enum inside InjurySet's readonly record struct correctly for both serialization and deserialization.
