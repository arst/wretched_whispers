## Gaps Summary

No gaps. All must-haves across all three plans are verified. The phase goal — "fix critical bugs and structural design issues in the domain layer" — is fully achieved:

- **Critical bug 1 (ScrollRestrictionPolicy):** Fixed. The `!` negation is present at Character.cs:342.
- **Critical bug 2 (Encounter public setters):** Fixed. Both `IsStarted` and `IsEnded` have `private set` with `[JsonInclude]`.
- **Entity sealing:** All 6 unsealed entities (4 BrokenOutcome subtypes, Adversary, Campaign) are now `sealed`.
- **InjuryKind/InjurySet foundation:** Created, tested with 15 tests, correctly models the Mork Borg broken table.
- **Character decomposition:** InjurySet integrated, ArmorTier strategy, Inventory sealed class, Abilities immutable.
- **Aggregate boundary:** CharacterPlugin routes all inventory operations through Character delegate methods.
- **Serialization:** InjurySet round-trips through both JSON (JsonSerializationTests) and SQLite (CharacterRoundTripTests).

One minor deviation from plan spec: `GetPresencePenalty()` and `GetAgilityPenaltyDice()` were split into dice-returning methods (`GetPresencePenaltyDice()`, `GetAgilityPenaltyDice()`) rather than fixed-int returns, to accurately match the existing Character.Challenge dice-based penalty logic. This is a correctness improvement over the plan's approximation.
